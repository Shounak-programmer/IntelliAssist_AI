AI Ethics and Responsible AI

This sample educational document was created for testing IntelliAssist
AI.

1. Bias and Fairness

AI systems can reproduce or amplify patterns contained in their training
and evaluation data. Bias may appear when some groups are
underrepresented, when historical decisions contain unfair patterns, or
when labels and measurements are poorly designed. Fairness evaluation
should consider the intended use of the system and the consequences of
errors.

2. Privacy

AI applications may process personal, confidential, or proprietary
information. Developers should minimize unnecessary data collection,
protect stored information, control access, and understand how external
model APIs handle submitted data. Sensitive documents should not be
uploaded to a service without appropriate authorization and safeguards.

3. Transparency and Explainability

Users should have enough information to understand what an AI system is
doing and what its limitations are. For document question answering,
displaying the retrieved source document and page or chunk can improve
transparency because users can inspect the evidence behind an answer.

4. Accountability

AI systems should have clearly defined ownership and evaluation
processes. Human oversight remains important for high-impact decisions.
Teams should monitor failures, document system behavior, and establish
procedures for correcting problems.

5. Copyright and Data Ownership

Training and application data may be subject to copyright, licensing,
privacy, or contractual restrictions. Developers should use material
they are authorized to process and should understand the rules
applicable to their jurisdiction and use case.

6. Responsible Use of Generative AI

Generative AI should be used with appropriate safeguards. Applications
should reduce unsupported claims, protect confidential information, test
outputs, and provide users with a way to identify or challenge incorrect
responses.

7. Ethical Considerations for Document Assistants

A document assistant should clearly communicate which documents it can
access, avoid presenting unsupported claims as facts, and preserve
source metadata when possible. Citation display is useful because it
allows users to verify the answer against the underlying material.
